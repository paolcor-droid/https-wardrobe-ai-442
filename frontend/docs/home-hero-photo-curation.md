# LUMIÈRE home editorial photography — curation and clearance

The home hero uses 15 specifically sourced free Unsplash photographs, listed in `frontend/src/components/ChatEmpty.tsx` with source page, category and theme. Source pages identify their images as available under the free Unsplash License (not Unsplash+). The source URLs and IDs can be replaced one entry at a time. The existing 900 ms image transition and persisted next-image rotation are retained.

## Editorial brief
Aim for the look of understated high-end fashion **without implying any association with a luxury brand**. Use premium textures (linen, wool, fine knitwear), tailoring, clean architecture, natural light and restrained styling. Prioritise visible complete outfits and adequate negative space near the lower text overlay. Avoid visible brand logos, shopping-bag/product close-ups, generic retail racks, heavy filters, excessively busy backgrounds, or wedding-only images. Respect diverse ages and appearances. Target a balanced 5 women / 5 men / 5 mixed/couples subjects; balance summer, evening and smart casual within those groups.

## Licensing and pre-launch clearance
- Unsplash's standard licence permits commercial use of its free photos: https://unsplash.com/license
- **Photo copyright is not the only clearance required.** Unsplash warns that recognizable people, visible brands, or artworks can involve separate rights: https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612315-can-i-use-unsplash-images-for-personal-or-commercial-projects and https://help.unsplash.com/en/articles/2612332-what-do-you-mean-by-compiling-images-to-replicate-a-similar-or-competing-service
- Review each photo with marketing/legal before public release, including whether model releases are appropriate for this promotional use. Do not imply endorsement from Prada, Armani, Loro Piana or the photographed subjects.
- Save a dated record of the individual photo source page, author and usage terms for the final approved set. If model or brand rights cannot be cleared, replace the photo with one from an appropriately model-released commercial stock library.
- Test all 15 redirecting download URLs in mobile and browser previews, including load time and cropping; replace broken links with approved, hosted static assets. **Code update does not constitute live URL validation or rights clearance.**
